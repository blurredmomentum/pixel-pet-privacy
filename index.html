Pixel Pet Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Pixel Pet does not collect, transmit, or share any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no network requests, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not use the microphone or the camera; the music and sounds are files bundled inside the app. The day/night lighting, weather and seasonal decorations come from your device's clock and date only; nothing is looked up online.

What the app stores, and where

Everything stays on your device, in the app's own local storage (key pixelpet.save.v1). Nothing is sent anywhere. The save holds:

  • Your pet: its name, stats (Fed, Joy, Energy), nap state, growth stage and cared days, and its personality traits (two of six, picked when it hatches).
  • Progress: your check-in streak, coins and today's coin counters, items you own and have on (outfits, room decor, wallpaper and flooring), best mini-game scores, and the pet's journal of milestones.
  • Garden and tricks: what's planted in each window-box plot and on which days it was watered, your seeds and treats, and how far each trick has been practised.
  • Badges and stats: which badges you've earned and when, and simple counters behind them (times petted, photos saved to your device, harvests, treats fed, trick practices and performances, seasons the app was opened in).
  • Settings: sound on/off, music and sound volumes, haptics, seasonal decor, and the reminder on/off and time.

If a save ever can't be read (for example it was damaged, or it came from a newer version of the app), the app keeps an unchanged copy of it on the device, so it isn't lost, and starts a new one. The first copy (pixelpet.save.bak) is never overwritten; if it happens again with a different save, that one is kept as well (pixelpet.save.bak.<time>), up to three copies in all, dropping the oldest of the later ones first. A save from a newer version is never overwritten.

Deleting your data

  • Settings > Reset everything (asks twice) erases your pet, coins, items, wallpaper and flooring, garden, tricks, badges, stats, best scores, journal and streak, and any backup copies. Your settings are kept (sound, volumes, haptics, seasonal decor and the reminder time), and any scheduled reminder is cancelled because there's no pet to remind you about.
  • Deleting the app removes everything, settings included.

Optional permissions

Both are off until you use the feature, and the app works fully without them.

  • Photos (add only). Photo mode makes a picture of your pet on your device. On iPhone, the first tap on Save to Photos asks for add-only access; Pixel Pet only adds the picture you just took and never reads, browses or uploads your photo library. On Android 11 and later no permission is needed to save; on Android 10 the system asks for storage access the first time. The app does not ask for permission to read your photos on any platform. Sharing a photo uses the system share sheet, and you choose where it goes.
  • Notifications. Asked only when you turn on the daily reminder in Settings (it's off by default). Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push service, push token or server is involved (the app is built without the push notifications entitlement). Turning the reminder off cancels them.

Children

Pixel Pet collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
