Quick answers

  • Where is my pet saved? Only on your device. There are no accounts, so we can't see or restore it for you.
  • Starting over: Settings > Reset everything (it asks twice). Your settings are kept.
  • Reminders: turn the daily reminder on or off in Settings. If notifications are off for the app, allow them in your phone's Settings.
  • Saving photos: Photo mode > Save. On iPhone it asks for add-only Photos access the first time.

Privacy: see the privacy policy. In short, Pixel Pet collects nothing.
